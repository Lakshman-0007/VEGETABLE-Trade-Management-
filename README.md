🥦 Vegetable Trade Management & Price Prediction System
📌 Overview
I built this web application for my father’s vegetable trading business.
He manages daily imports from farmers and exports to markets. Earlier, all records were written in notebooks, which made it difficult to track history, calculate profits accurately, and maintain data safely. Sometimes records were misplaced, and checking past transactions took a lot of time.
This system digitizes his entire workflow and makes daily operations simple, accurate, and secure.
🎯 Why I Built This
My father was struggling with:
Maintaining daily handwritten records
Calculating total costs and profits manually
Tracking past 2–3 months of transactions
Risk of losing notebooks or damaging records
Difficulty in sending clear transaction details to farmers
To solve this, I developed a web-based management system that automates calculations, stores historical data safely, and generates reports in PDF format.
🚀 What This System Does
🔹 Import Management
Add vegetables purchased from farmers
Enter multiple weight entries
Automatically calculate total weight and cost
Store complete transaction history
🔹 Export Management
Record selling price in markets
Add delivery and extra charges
Automatically calculate profit or loss
🔹 Data Storage
Secure database storage
View past records by date or vegetable
Maintain 2–3 months (or more) of historical data
🔹 PDF Report Generation
Generate farmer transaction reports as PDF
Clear and professional format
Easy to send and share for transparency
